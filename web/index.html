Softer printable TPU wins; no foam is required. Within printable grades the relationship is monotonic, so the choice trades directly against print difficulty — 70–80A is the practical compromise. Shore hardness was converted to modulus with an empirical relation carrying roughly ±30% scatter; a compression test on a printed coupon would replace it.
