What this can and cannot tell you. Pose detection, contact mechanics and geometry are valid for any video. The loading prediction is not — that model was trained on one fixed camera, in one room, with shod feet. On your footage it is out of distribution, and the pressure it reports is illustrative of the pipeline, not a measurement of the person in the clip.

Filament selection

GradeBulk modulusPeak pressure vs 95APrintability
Softer printable TPU wins; no foam required. Within printable grades the relationship is monotonic, so the choice trades directly against print difficulty — 70–80A is the practical compromise. Shore hardness was converted to modulus with an empirical relation carrying roughly ±30% scatter; a compression test on a printed coupon would replace it.
